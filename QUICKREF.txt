🎯 TRAINING COMMANDS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Train on conversation:    ./train-ai.sh conversation
  Train on coding:          ./train-ai.sh coding
  Train on minecraft:       ./train-ai.sh minecraft
  Train on everything:      ./train-ai.sh all (RECOMMENDED)
  
  Custom epochs:            EPOCHS=200 ./train-ai.sh all

🔧 CONFIGURATION
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Environment:
    PORT=8788              Server port
    AI_MODE=general        Mode selection
    EPOCHS=600             Training iterations
    USE_EMBEDDINGS=true    Semantic search
  
  Training config:  ai-core/training/train_model.py
  - BLOCK_SIZE: 256       Context window
  - BATCH_SIZE: 16        Batch size
  - EMBED_DIM: 192        Model dimension
  - NUM_HEADS: 6          Attention heads
  - NUM_LAYERS: 6         Transformer layers
